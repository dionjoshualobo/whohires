<div class="slide-number">02 / 06</div>
<div class="eyebrow">02 · Contribution</div>
<h2 style="margin-top: 0.7rem;">You do not have to code<br /><span class="accent">to be useful.</span></h2>
<p class="kicker" style="margin-top: 0.7rem;">Most project friction lives around the codebase: unclear docs, unreproduced bugs, confusing flows and unanswered questions.</p>

<div class="card-grid">
  <div class="card"><div class="num">01 · DOCUMENT</div><h3>Technical writing</h3><p>Fix setup steps, write tutorials, explain a confusing feature.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><div class="num">02 · LOCALIZE</div><h3>Translation</h3><p>Make interfaces and guides useful to more languages and communities.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><div class="num">03 · DESIGN</div><h3>UX &amp; accessibility</h3><p>Audit user journeys, create mockups and remove a11y barriers.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><div class="num">04 · VERIFY</div><h3>Testing &amp; triage</h3><p>Reproduce bugs, request missing logs and verify fixes across systems.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><div class="num">05 · CONNECT</div><h3>Community &amp; DevRel</h3><p>Answer questions, welcome newcomers and share release updates.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><div class="num">06 · DISCOVER</div><h3>Research &amp; product</h3><p>Collect feedback, propose features and help prioritize real needs.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="quote">Your first contribution does not have to be a pull request. It could be a bug report that saves a developer <em>three hours</em>.</div>

<div class="slide-number">05 / 06</div>
<div class="eyebrow">05 · Three routes in</div>
<h2 style="margin-top: 0.7rem;">Start small.<br /><span class="accent">Stay visible.</span></h2>

<div class="pathways">
  <div class="pathway">
    <div class="eyebrow" style="color: var(--blue);">ROUTE A</div>
    <h3>Contribute directly</h3>
    <p>Pick something you already use.</p>
    <ul><li>Read <code>CONTRIBUTING.md</code></li><li>Find docs, triage or beginner issues</li><li>Join the project’s community</li></ul>
  </div>
  <div class="pathway">
    <div class="eyebrow" style="color: var(--pink);">ROUTE B</div>
    <h3>Use a mentorship</h3>
    <p>Get structure, feedback and a stipend.</p>
    <ul><li>GSoC: mentored projects</li><li>LFX: Linux Foundation ecosystem</li><li>Outreachy: code and non-code</li></ul>
  </div>
  <div class="pathway">
    <div class="eyebrow" style="color: var(--orange);">ROUTE C</div>
    <h3>Build a reputation</h3>
    <p>Become someone the community trusts.</p>
    <ul><li>Contribute consistently</li><li>Review, discuss and help newcomers</li><li>Apply where your work is relevant</li></ul>
  </div>
</div>
